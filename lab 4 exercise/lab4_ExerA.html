ABOUT TERENGGANU

Terengganu is a beautiful state located on the east coast of Peninsular Malaysia. It is famous for its beautiful islands, stunning beaches, waterfalls, lakes and interesting tourist attractions. Discover the hidden beauty and unforgettable experiences of Terengganu.

EXPLORE BEAUTIFUL PLACES

Discover the amazing destinations in Terengganu

Pulau Redang

Famous for its crystal-clear water, white sandy beaches and marine life.

Pulau Perhentian

A tropical paradise perfect for snorkelling, diving and relaxing.

Tasik Kenyir

Explore Malaysia's largest man-made lake surrounded by nature.

Masjid Kristal

An iconic mosque known for its beautiful glass and steel architecture.

Drawbridge Kuala Terengganu

A famous landmark offering beautiful views of Kuala Terengganu.

Air Terjun Sekayu

A beautiful waterfall surrounded by green tropical forests.
