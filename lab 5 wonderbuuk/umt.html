Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university focuses on marine science, fisheries, aquatic science and environmental studies.

UMT provides higher education opportunities and encourages research and innovation in various fields. The university aims to produce knowledgeable and skilled graduates who contribute to society and the environment.

About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu is committed to academic excellence, research and innovation. It offers various academic programmes to prepare students for their future careers.
